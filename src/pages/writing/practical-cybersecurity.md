## Start with what can go wrong

Security becomes more concrete when you ask what an attacker can reach, what they can change, what they can steal, and what evidence remains afterward.

## Then reduce the blast radius

Strong security is rarely one magic control. It is layers: least privilege, good defaults, visibility, sensible architecture, and practiced response.
