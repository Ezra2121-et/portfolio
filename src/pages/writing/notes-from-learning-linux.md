## The short version

Linux gets more interesting when the command line stops feeling like a separate world and starts feeling like the system itself.

## What I am practicing

- Files, permissions, users, and processes
- Networking tools and service inspection
- Shell scripting for repetitive tasks
- Reading logs instead of guessing

## Why I am writing this down

The fastest way I forget something is to understand it once and never use it again. These notes are a place to keep the useful parts.
